# Put everything together

Imagine you're building a RAG-based college assistant in Node.js.

Here's how the concepts you just learned fit together:

|Concept|What it means for your application|
|---|---|
|Tokens|Control input/output usage and estimate costs.|
|Context window|Ensure the question, instructions, history, and retrieved documents fit.|
|Temperature|Influence how consistently or variably the answer is generated.|
|Inference|The model processes the request and generates a response.|
|Limitations|Verify important information, validate outputs, and don't assume the model can perform actions by itself.|
